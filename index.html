AutoDoc

An open-source Granola alternative for Mac and Windows, with local AI processing and timestamped video playback.

Record what was said and shown. AutoDoc turns meeting audio into editable notes, while the screen recording lets you replay the moment behind a timestamp. Transcription, notes, and Ask AI run on your computer, not through a cloud AI service.

No meeting bot, AutoDoc account, or AI API key required.

Core features

Capture the meeting. Keep the meeting.

AutoDoc records screen video and meeting audio, creates editable notes, and lets you replay a timestamp or ask a question.

01 Detect

AutoDoc notices an active meeting and asks before recording.

02 Record

Screen video, mic, and system audio are captured together from your device.

03 Review

Notes and transcript stay linked to the recording — the screen context is one click away.

Meeting languages

Meetings in your language.

Get transcripts, editable notes, and Ask AI answers in your selected meeting language. AutoDoc supports 27 languages, including Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Korean. Choose your language during setup or in Settings. Processing stays on your computer.

Language availability depends on your hardware. 8 GB Macs and lower-spec Windows PCs remain English-only. See supported languages and requirements.

Notes

Notes you can work with.

Start with a summary, then explore the conversation by topic. Edit the details, jump from an available timestamp to the recording, or export your notes as PDF, Word, or Markdown.

Search

Find the exact moment.

Search your transcripts and notes, then open the matching meeting to review the context.

Ask AI

Ask your meetings.

Ask questions across past meetings and review answers grounded in your meeting history. Retrieval and responses run locally through Ollama.

Review

The full picture.

Open the recording in the Transcript tab, or select an available timestamp in your notes to revisit what was said and shown. Microphone audio is labeled Me and combined system audio is labeled Them. AutoDoc does not identify each remote participant.

Upcoming

Your calendar, ready to record.

Connect Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook to see upcoming meetings, auto-name recordings, and choose whether a meeting records once, for a whole recurring series, or only when you say so. Detection offers to record. A meeting you already set to record once or for the series can start without another prompt.

Comparison

Looking for a Granola alternative?

AutoDoc is built around local AI processing and recordings you can revisit. Transcription, notes, and Ask AI run on your computer, and available timestamps take you back to the screen recording. It is free and open source, with ready-to-use Mac and Windows installers.

Local AI needs local resources: allow roughly 10 GB for initial setup and extra space for recordings. 8 GB RAM is supported; 16 GB or more is recommended.
